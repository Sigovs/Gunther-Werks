# CONCEPT C — Gunther Werks Miami home page: brand presentation (25 Oct 2026)

Direction set by Alex after reviewing drafts A (prototype/home) and B (prototype/home-b), which stay as drafts:
"I like what we did on Pagani, Czinger and Lambo — a very clear message, brand presentation. Look at rivian.com and racing.porsche.com. Concept first, as an image: the whole page with every section we need."

## What the reference sites teach (studied 25 Oct, scroll-stop sheets in scratchpad/refstops)
- **Czinger Miami / Lamborghini Miami / Pagani of Miami (ours):** the first screen says who we are — "<Brand> Miami", official dealer — with two actions. Each model gets its own full screen: big model name, the car large in profile, a spec plate, Inquire + Explore. Then brand depth (engineering, founder, records), then the dealer (in the showroom / pre-owned, service, visit, form), then a structured footer.
- **rivian.com:** a giant model name set behind a clean side-profile car on a light ground; the cars slide sideways from model to model; one line + two buttons under each.
- **racing.porsche.com:** a red studio where a silhouette is lit into the car, with model tabs; a heritage strip with huge years; a journal strip; statement lines set large on white.

## The message, in one line
**Gunther Werks Miami — the official Gunther Werks dealer in Miami. Four remastered 993s, built in California, shown and sold by Prestige Imports.**
