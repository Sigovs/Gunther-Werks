# CONCEPT D — Gunther Werks Miami home page, in Gunther Werks' own language (25 Oct 2026)

**The idea, in one sentence:** the page is Gunther Werks' own catalogue — each program lit in its own "Active Programs" cove with its logotype set huge behind the car, then the tiered collection with GW's badges, then the commission itself, step by step from the owner's application to the billet key, all leading to "Begin your Gunther Werks" with Javier Roque in Miami.

Static concept image (no JS). Two authored compositions: 1440×900 and 1920×1080 (`@media (min-width:1680px)` raises `--t` 1.2, `--k` 1.3, gutter 80→120). Mobile (390) deliberately not built.
Renders: `CONCEPT-D-1440.png`, `CONCEPT-D-1920.png` (+ `-half.jpg`) are the 15 viewport screens stitched top to bottom, each with the fixed nav in the state it has on that screen; per-screen PNGs in `sections/1440|1920/`.

## What changed from C (Alex, on C)
- **Models:** no light ground and no dark spec plate. Each program sits in GW's own dark-cove photograph from its home "Active Programs" set (F-26 red, Turbo amber, GWR blue, GWX navy with red rim). The car is masked out of the same frame (rembg isnet), so **GW's own model logotype** sits between the cove and the car — large name behind a big car, in GW's lettering, not ours. Stats are GW's block: Bebas figure, JetBrains Mono unit, small label.
- **Commission journey added** (3 screens + the application), sourced from GW's site and press.
- **Finish** taken from racing.porsche.com (Alex's craft bar): a small floating pill nav, one thing per screen, sentence-case statements, pill tabs over the lit car, rounded panels and cards with a corner arrow, heritage years over B/W photographs.
- **Type** (Alex, 25 Oct): Instrument Sans at 88% width for all text (statements 400, sentence case), Bebas Neue for figures only, JetBrains Mono only for GW-style units and badges. **Buttons:** thin 1px outline, 40 px, small tracked label; at most one slim filled primary per screen. **Copy:** GW's own words for every statement.

## Facts and their sources (content-provenance)
- F-26 1,000 hp is GW's dated release figure (F26-PR, 15 Aug 2025) and the date is printed in the render; 1,067 (site hero) is not shown. GWX 440 hp / 320 lb-ft from GWX-PR (13 Aug 2025), date printed. Turbo shows no horsepower (850 site vs 840 fact sheet). No weights where GW contradicts itself (F-26, GXR-EVO). No prices anywhere.
- Journey: purchase screening and its questions, "Please allow 48 business hours for a response" (owners-application); "Every program is built on a Porsche 993" (STORY, CI §2); bare-metal disassembly (GW program copy); "over 90%" in-house (BG-PDF); delivery dinner at the Huntington Beach facility, billet key, first start, warranty 5 years / 100,000 miles incl. future upgrades, remains with the vehicle (OWN); PSP succession cover "topped to a minimum of 3 years or 100,000 miles" (PSP pages).
- Customization is written as "with the Gunther Werks team" (GW: "our team will guide you through specification"); GW does not say "in person", so the page does not.
- Dealer: Prestige Imports · 15050 Biscayne Blvd, North Miami Beach, FL 33181 · Javier Roque · (305) 783-6493 · Javier@PrestigeImports.com (GW /dealer).

## Working prototype (25 Oct 2026) — "Billet & Gauge" (UI-D.md, CD-REVIEW.md)

Run: serve `C:\____WORK\PRESTIGE` (e.g. `python -m http.server 8790`) and open `/PRESTIGE_GUNTHER/prototype/concept-d/index.html`. Files: `index.html`, `concept-d.css`, `concept-d.js`, `vendor/` (GSAP + ScrollTrigger + Lenis, copied from prototype/home). The static renders above (`CONCEPT-D-*.png`, `sections/`) are the earlier concept image and are kept unchanged; QA shots of the prototype are in `_shots/build/<width>/`.

**What changed from the concept image (CD-REVIEW §2.3 P1):** all Porsche chrome removed — centred GW lockup over a tonal scrim with links on a scale groove and GW's orange needle; billet primary / stroke-draw secondary buttons; hairline-arrow go-links; tick-scale switchers; captions under photos; no pills, no blur, no corner boxes, no glass. Hero = "Gunther Werks Miami. / Built in California." ("Shown in Miami." deleted). Programs = one pinned stage (the peak). Commission opens with GW's "Bespoke by design." on a graphite plate docked into the HQ photograph, one numbering system (the step scale). Our story = key photo + 2017→2026 timeline scale. Press = plates with publication names in type (no logos). Form = grooved fields, detent scale for track experience, plain radios, Registration state select, consent checkbox, loading / error / success states. Provenance re-cropped. Type floor 14 px.

**Motion (desktop ≥1024):** Lenis + ScrollTrigger. The programs stage pins for ~4 screens: each cove goes dark, the silhouettes change places, the next cove is re-lit, GW's logotype settles behind a car that does not move; the switcher needle follows scroll and the odometer rolls at each station; tabs jump to a station. The commission pins for 1.1 screens while the needle advances 01→03 and each step is read in turn. Statements are cut in line by line; images drift inside their frames; build frames arrive at different heights; Goodwood opens out; the footer drum rolls GW's tagline once (innovation → pleasure). Collection: selecting a car = feathered colour reveal, one satin highlight along the body, a faint floor reflection, the needle moves (no cones). Phone/tablet: the same moments, no pins, no parallax. Reduced motion: the static composition with four stacked program screens; needles and digits jump.

**Verified (Playwright + system Chrome):** 1920, 1440, 1024, 768, 430, 390, 360 — horizontal overflow 0 and console errors 0 at every stop; reduced-motion pass at 1440. Contrast on the composited render (text hidden vs shown, 5th percentile of the pixels under each run) at 1440, 1920 and 390, including all four stage states: 873 runs, all pass except the two radio labels whose measured box includes the orange selected dot (text itself 18:1). Text over a car's silhouette: 0% coverage on all four program screens at 1440 and 1920. Interactions forward/back: stage tabs (GWR → F-26), Program details drawer (focus to heading, Esc returns focus), collection by click and arrow keys, timeline next/2026, form errors → loading → success, menu sheet (≤1279: opens, page inert, Esc returns focus).

**Fixes after the coordinator's wheel-scroll QA (25 Oct 2026):**
- Header: once the page moves (scrollY > 24) the header sits on a solid graphite band with the engraved groove at its foot; on the first hero screen it stays on the scrim. Every section's first line clears the header by ≥ 32 px (1440: 64 px everywhere; 390: 32 px or more).
- Programs stage: every rest position is a lit program. A change is a short re-light of the next cove over the still-lit previous one (old light dips to .72 for ~10% of a program's scroll span, composite light never below 0.74, one logotype at a time). Silhouettes and the dark phase are gone. Phone: no scrubbed darkness; a one-time re-light when each program arrives.
- Phone voids: the program cove is lifted into the top of its screen, flow sections lose their forced 100svh, the hero is 88svh. Largest near-black band in any 390 wheel frame: 136 px.
