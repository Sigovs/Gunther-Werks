05Our story

Every door that closed.

When Peter Nam set out to create Gunther Werks, investors and industry veterans said it couldn’t be done. Nearly all turned him away. The company key was designed in an “X” pattern — a symbol of every door that closed.

The name pays tribute to Gunther Wendt, the NASA engineer known for his exacting standards during the Apollo program.
