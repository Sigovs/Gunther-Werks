1 · Programs, revised“Four programs. One 993.”

The order is unchanged: choose, see, understand, enquire.

  • The chapter is dark. The car stands on a light, neutral studio plate, with one ground recipe and one contact shadow for every car.
  • Selecting a program replaces the car directly. There is no ghost, outline or second car, and no hover-only state.
  • The supporting sentence is gone.

Interaction

  • Mouse: click a name.
  • Keyboard: ←/→ between tabs, Home/End.
  • Touch: tap a segment, or swipe the plate.
  • The new car replaces the old in place: the old is removed and the new fades up from the studio ground in 180 ms, so two cars are never on screen. Reduced motion: an instant swap.
  • Scroll never changes the program.

Studio plate

  • A light neutral cove (#f5f4f1 → #e0ded9), 20 px radius.
  • The car is registered on its measured tyre contact points, so the wheelbase, scale and floor line are identical for all eight.
  • One contact shadow is drawn on the shared floor line: a tight shadow under each tyre plus a soft body shadow. No glow, rim light or outline.

Cutout clean-up (alpha only)

  • GW's cutouts carry floor shadows baked in for their dark site. On a light floor they double up with the contact shadow.
  • For every car, the baked shadow below the tyre contact line is removed.
  • Brundle also had a hard black bar along the floor, an opaque strip the tyres sit in. Only near-black pixels in that strip, outside the measured tyre discs, were removed.
  • The car itself is untouched: colour, angle, crop and paint.

Facts (the same three per program)

  • F-26: 1,000 hp, 4.0 L twin-turbo, slantnose after the 930 Flachbau.
  • Turbo: 850 hp in track mode, 4.0 L twin-turbo, hydraulic nose lift.
  • GWR: 500+ hp at 9,000 rpm, 4.0 L naturally aspirated, S-duct hood and ventilated ducktail.
  • GWX: 440 hp, second-generation 4.0 L, Candy Purple exclusive.
  • Sources are GW programme pages and releases. F-26 power follows the 15 Aug 2025 release; GW's site also says 1,067 hp.

Known limits

  • GW publishes the Turbo and Thermal profiles only at about 1,300 px. Both are softer than the others at 1440 and on retina.
  • The F-26 and GWX wing tips touch the top edge of GW's own files. Not visible at these sizes.
  • The GXR-EVO keeps GW's own baked shadow, which is darker than the others. Its body is black carbon, so a colour-based clean-up would risk eating the car.

Lineup checkEvery car at its actual display size, shown at 2×

The four programs at their 1440 stage size (plate 1296×414 CSS px); the four special editions at their 1440 tile size (309×140). Wheels, tyres and lower body are visible on the ground in every frame.

3 · Contact, simplified“Start in Miami.”

One purpose and one action. The section is a heading, one line and “Make an enquiry”. Clicking opens a compact form in place, where the interest is a choice inside the form, not three explanatory blocks. There is no photograph, address, phone, email, specialist credit or caption.

Behaviour

  • “Make an enquiry” (and every “Enquire about the …” button) opens the form here, with the interest and program preselected.
  • “Close” returns to the button.
  • The form states are F's: empty, focus, field error, sending, sent, and failed with the typed text kept.

Copy
