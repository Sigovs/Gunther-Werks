# Cosmos references for Gunther Werks Miami (v2)

Source: https://www.cosmos.so/explore, browsed logged out on 25 Oct 2026 with Playwright (Chrome, 1440–1600 px).
Shots: `shots/` (`el-*` = Cosmos element page, `vid-*` = six frames from a Cosmos video, `site-*` = live website, `grid-*` = numbered search or collection grid, `cosmos-*` = Cosmos's own pages).

**Short verdict.** Cosmos is a mood board, not a web gallery. Its **automotive photography and lighting** material is strong. Its **automotive web design** material is poor: searches for "automotive website" and "car website" return mostly Dribbble concepts (`grid-automotive_website_p0.jpg`, `grid-car_website_p0.jpg`), and none of them should be copied. The useful web ideas come from non-car sites (Making Software, Panter&Tourron, Anduril, Loewe) and from short motion clips that designers uploaded. Several "dark studio car" results are AI renders, and one even shows the prompt text in the image. They were left out.

This file adds to `../awwwards/AWWWARDS_REFS.md` and does not repeat it. Scout, ESPN F1, Charge Cars, EMO Altar II and iPad Pro are covered there.

## 2. Picks by page section

Each pick gives the Cosmos URL, what it shows, and the move it suggests for our page. "Strong / useful / narrow" is my rating.

### Hero → lineup transition

**1. TB-LAB 008 beauty shot: strong**
https://www.cosmos.so/e/2145795187 · `el-orange-horizon-silhouette.png`, `vid-tblab.jpg`
A car in full silhouette on black. One horizontal band of orange light sits behind it at wheel height. Over three seconds the band widens from a hairline to a wide glow, the glossy floor picks up the reflection, and the body's shape appears only through its outline.
**Move:** this is our cove plus one coloured rim light, turned into a timeline. Pin the hero and let the scroll **widen the rim-light band**: hairline → glow → full cove. The car first reads as an outline and then as an object. The end state of the band can be the start of the lineup (more cars appear in the same light). The light is per program (F-26 / Turbo / GWR / GWX colour). *Caveat: the source is a CGI concept. Take the light choreography only; GW's own studio stills or film have to carry it.*

**2. Adam Fussell for British Vogue, Porsche under a round softbox: strong**
https://www.cosmos.so/e/2089848629 · `el-studio-softbox-911.png`
A 911 Targa, small and low in the frame. A single huge round overhead softbox hangs above it in a dark blue-grey cove. The car takes about 15% of the frame. The light fixture is the composition.
**Move:** first-screen scale. The car is small and the **light source is visible as a shape in the frame**. The page's air comes from the studio itself, not from padding. It also gives a clean end frame for reduced motion.

**3. The Art of Empty Space (collection): useful**
https://www.cosmos.so/spaces/the-art-of-empty-space · `grid-C_the_art_of_empty_space_p0.jpg`, `el-empty-space-figure.png` (Matt Lambert, https://www.cosmos.so/e/1183165332)
Light-art and architecture rooms: glowing walls, light discs reflected on concrete, and one figure against a field of light.
**Move:** a vocabulary for **staging between the cars**: a light wall, a reflected disc, one human figure for scale. Use it for the transition from hero to lineup, or as the backdrop of the Miami showroom / ownership block, so the dealer space reads as a gallery and not a lot.

**4. ULTRACE, overhead light box in haze: useful (photo reference)**
https://www.cosmos.so/e/204448446 · `el-studio-overhead-box.png`
A rectangular overhead light box fills the top of the frame. Haze makes the light volume visible, and a dark sedan sits on a glossy floor below.
**Move:** a reference for the haze in our cove. The light should have **volume** (a visible column), not only a highlight on the car.

### Pinned model step-throughs with callouts

**5. Making Software (live site): strong**
Cosmos: https://www.cosmos.so/e/1291074111 → https://www.makingsoftware.com/ · `site-makingsoftware-1440-*.png`
Dan Hollick's reference manual. Each figure is an exploded line drawing (floppy disk, CRT, hard drive) with **thin leader lines ending in small mono labels** ("TOP SHELL", "WRITE PROTECT TAB"). Figures carry a "FIG_001 [ 3.5" FLOPPY DISK ] © 1986" header, and the prose column runs beside them.
**Move:** the grammar for the callouts on the model pins. Use hairline leaders from a precise point on the car to a mono label, plus a "FIG / program / year" header per pinned frame. Draw the callouts **on a scrubbed timeline, one leader at a time**. Do not take the blue blueprint colour; use near-white on our dark cove.

**6. Car brochures: the model name as architecture: useful**
911 spread: https://www.cosmos.so/e/1015392496 (`el-brochure-911-numeral.png`) · 992 spread: https://www.cosmos.so/e/1367879788 (`el-brochure-992.png`)
Print spreads where the model number is set huge and condensed, and the car crosses in front of the numerals.
**Move:** for each pinned model, the designation (F-26, GWR, GWX, Turbo) can be a quiet **structural** element that the car sits in front of, revealed on scroll. Only if it stays tonal (dark on dark) and never fights the car. *Risk: the awwwards notes already rejected Scout's "giant overlapping headlines". Use this only as a low-contrast background plane, never as the headline.*

**7. "Turbo – TYPE 930 (3.0L)" poster: narrow**
https://www.cosmos.so/e/13472717 · `el-turbo-poster-spec.png`
A side profile on a flat colour, the model script above, and one line of widely tracked spec text below ("T Y P E 9 3 0 (3.0L)").
**Move:** the **spec plate** under each pinned car: one tracked mono line (program · base · displacement). No table. Use only figures GW has confirmed.

### Model rotating through angles on scroll

**8. Lightmap / HDR Light Studio, BMW M4 strip-light study: strong**
https://www.cosmos.so/e/718245089 · `el-strip-light-sweep.png`
Six frames of the same side profile. In each, one strip light moves to a new position in the cove (shown in the right column), and the highlight line runs along the body.
**Move:** "rotation" does not have to mean turning the car. The scroll can **move the light across a fixed car**, so the specular line travels along the flank and reveals the surfaces one at a time. It is cheaper than a 360° spin, looks more like an atelier, and works with a handful of stills shot in the same position (a crossfade between light positions). *CGI demo; GW would need to shoot the light positions.*

**9. elferspot, 930 Turbo walkaround (Porsche Museum): useful**
https://www.cosmos.so/e/2119346222 · `vid-turbo.jpg`
A phone walkaround with an editorial order: three-quarter front → head-on → **headlight macro** → rear → **rear light-bar macro** → back to three-quarter. The car sits on a black glossy floor in a white architectural space.
**Move:** a **shot list** for the angle sequence: the wide view alternates with one macro detail per angle, and the sequence ends where it began. Map the scroll to these steps (wide / detail / wide / detail).

**10. @johnrandall / Automotive (cluster): useful**
https://www.cosmos.so/johnrandall/automotive · `grid-C_jr_automotive_p0.jpg`, `el-jr-911-topview.png` (https://www.cosmos.so/e/283763873), `el-jr-vertical-car.png` (https://www.cosmos.so/e/1993014472), `el-jr-silhouette.png` (https://www.cosmos.so/e/981010280)
White-seamless catalogue work at unusual angles: a straight-down top view of a 911, a full black side silhouette on pale grey, and cars turned 90° so they run vertically up a portrait frame.
**Move:** the **top view** is the angle GW's catalogue is missing. Add it as the last stop of the rotation (side → three-quarter → top). The vertical car is a real option for the **390 px** frame, but the earlier research warned against "standing the object on end". Treat it as one deliberate mobile frame, not a pattern.

### Overlapping sections and full-screen moments with parallax

**11. Thomas Hervé Studio, sliced-portrait website: strong**
https://www.cosmos.so/e/2042623192 · `el-slice-face.png`, `vid-slice.jpg`
A jewellery site. One large portrait continues **behind** several stacked white panels. Each panel shows a different band of the same face as you scroll, so the page reads as layers sliding over one continuous image.
**Move:** this is the overlapping-section idea done properly. Fix one full-bleed GW image (a car in the cove, or the golden-hour shot) and slide content panels over it, so the image appears in **bands between panels**. This gives parallax without moving the image itself.

**12. Anduril (live site): useful**
Cosmos: https://www.cosmos.so/e/1607391966 → https://www.anduril.com/ · `site-anduril-1440-*.png`
A full-bleed film hero with no headline (earth and sky moving). Then a dark mosaic of products at different tile sizes, each with only its name in the corner. Then quiet grey rows: a large name on the left, the image on the right.
**Move:** the order *film → mosaic of names → one row per product* matches our *hero → lineup → program rows*. Its product tiles carry the **name only**, which is the restraint our lineup cards need. *Keep the existing glass cards (approved, per memory) but keep their labels this bare.*

**13. Ilmar Pakkanen, 911 in white haze: useful (photo)**
https://www.cosmos.so/e/1677609499 · `el-911-fog.png`
A dark 993-era 911 cut off by the bottom edge. The top 80% of the frame is pale fog.
**Move:** a **full-screen moment with a built-in text field**. Headline and caption go in the fog, the car stays at the bottom edge, and the text never touches the car. Parallax: the car rises slightly while the type stays put.

**14. DIRTYALPINE, car in a teal shadow: narrow (photo)**
https://www.cosmos.so/e/1119651697 · `el-teal-shadow-car.png`
A small dark car almost lost in a deep teal field, cut by one diagonal of light.
**Move:** a **pause frame** between dense sections, where almost nothing is visible. It is a breath, not content.

### Craft macro mosaic

**15. Carbon fibre under raking light: useful**
https://www.cosmos.so/e/360647435 (`el-carbon-curl.png`) · https://www.cosmos.so/e/996230320 (`el-carbon-twill.png`) · `grid-carbon_fiber_p0.jpg`
A carbon sheet curving on pure black, and a close-up of twill weave lit at a grazing angle.
**Move:** **one hard grazing light per macro tile on black**. The weave is visible only where the light crosses it. Use it as the art direction for GW's own carbon macros and set the mosaic on black.

**16. CNC in the dark: useful**
https://www.cosmos.so/e/1224529294 (`el-cnc-radial.png`) · Buster + Punch https://www.cosmos.so/e/258536594 (`el-cnc-gold-vise.png`) · `grid-cnc_machining_p0.jpg`
The spindle over an aluminium block with a radial cut, and a brass part in a vise lit inside a black machine.
**Move:** frame the machine in darkness, with only the part lit, as the "billet / CNC" tile. A **short loop** (spindle and chips) can sit in one mosaic cell while the others stay still.

**17. Thomas Hervé Studio, jewellery row: useful**
https://www.cosmos.so/e/2064428600 · `el-jewel-row.png`, `vid-jewel.jpg`
Rings lined up macro on black in a single row. Then the layout switches between a full-width macro row and an off-grid editorial cluster of hands, faces and objects.
**Move:** the **mosaic rhythm**: one wide row of objects on black (billet key, shift knob, wheel nut, pedal), then an asymmetric cluster of hands at work. Do not use an even grid.

**18. LOEWE FOUNDATION Craft Prize (live site): useful**
Cosmos: https://www.cosmos.so/e/1812728253 → https://craftprizeexhibition.loewe.com/ · `site-loewe-craft-1440-*.png`
A film hero, then each piece shown **next to its maker standing beside it**, at the same scale.
**Move:** in the craft section, show **one part next to the hands or person who made it**. That proves the work is made by hand without saying so.

### Lifestyle and ownership large screens

**19. McLaren F1 under a giant softbox (Petar Delic): useful**
https://www.cosmos.so/e/1727746250 · `el-mclaren-cove.png`, plus Devynn Higa https://www.cosmos.so/e/327688205 (`el-r34-softbox-walk.png`)
A silver car in a white cove under a huge softbox, with the rig in shot. In the second image a person walks past the car under the light.
**Move:** **keep the rig in frame**. The studio becomes the story (commissioning and handover in Miami), and one person walking by gives scale. It bridges the white-seamless catalogue and the dark cove.

**20. Vintage Porsche ad, "How to beat the traffic home.": useful**
https://www.cosmos.so/e/1749326714 · `el-porsche-ad-traffic.png`
A stacked condensed headline, a small side-profile line drawing of the car, and two columns of witty copy on white.
**Move:** an **editorial register for ownership**: a confident one-line headline, the car small, and real prose underneath. Use it as a contrast block (light ground) between the dark sections. *Copy must be GW's own; do not pastiche the ad.*

### Horizontal commissions strip

**21. Thumbnails assemble into a filmstrip: useful**
https://www.cosmos.so/e/1542673428 · `el-thumb-strip.png`, `vid-strip.jpg`
Small thumbnails float scattered in a large white field, then snap into **one thin horizontal line** across the middle, then bend (and finally form a ring).
**Move:** open the commissions strip as **scattered builds that align into one rail** as the section pins, then pan the rail sideways. Stop at the straight line. *The ring step is a gimmick; drop it.*

**22. Cascading Slider: narrow**
https://www.cosmos.so/e/860832544 · `vid-cascade.jpg`
One large active card with neighbours squeezed into thin slices on both sides. It advances one card at a time and the caption sits inside the active card.
**Move:** the resting state of the strip: **one commission large, the next and previous as slivers**. Readable on mobile and needs no hover.

### Editorial news list

**23. Panter&Tourron (live site): strong**
Cosmos: https://www.cosmos.so/e/739082819 → https://pantertourron.com/ · `site-pantertourron-1440-*.png`
A black page with an **index table**: № · year · project · client · discipline · image count, in small type and long rows. One short studio statement sits at the top.
**Move:** News & Events as a **numbered index** (№ · date · type: press / event / build · headline · outlet), not cards. Opening a row can reveal its image. It looks like an archive, which fits a collector brand.

**24. Index Studio, dated list: useful**
https://www.cosmos.so/e/646157309 · `el-index-dated.png`
Rows of date, small thumbnail, title and short text, with a big year label ("Janv–Sept 2023", "2022") separating the groups.
**Move:** group the news **by season or year with a large quiet year marker**, with thumbnails kept small.

## 3. Skipped (and why)

- **"automotive website", "car website", "luxury website"**: Dribbble-style concepts ("INTRODUCING THE FUTURE", orange GT-R, red-dot CTAs). These are exactly the template look we are trying to get away from. Polestar 1/2 screens appear, but they are dated catalogue layouts.
- **"dark website", "website dark mode", "scroll animation website"**: SaaS dark mode with glows and glass, AI-template feel.
- **thetinypod.com**: a competent Apple clone (`site-tinypod-1440-*.png`), nothing GW-specific.
- **AUAR** (`site-auar-*`): a strong first screen (product in a grey studio), then a generic SaaS page.
- **3D ring gallery "Scroll"** (https://www.cosmos.so/e/126085823, `vid-ring.jpg`) and the **diagonal 3D cascade** (Pablo Escudero portfolio, https://www.cosmos.so/e/119063191, `vid-diag.jpg`): attention-grabbing devices that would bury the cars.
- **"car rim light"**: neon tuner/wheel aesthetic.
- **AI renders in "dark studio car"**: for example the overhead-box shot with the prompt printed in the frame, and the glowing-taillight hypercars.
- **@6/automotive**: hypercar Instagram.
- **Interaction Design (@mattjs)**: app micro-interactions, not relevant.

## 5. Not accessed / not verified

- **Logged-out limits.** Search and collection pages stop at about 45–60 tiles before a sign-up wall. "You may also like" on element pages is blurred. Nothing behind sign-in was opened, and no account was created.
- **Video frames** were sampled (six per clip) from Cosmos's own player. The motion was not tested on live sites, except for the first-screen and two scroll-position screenshots of the six live sites.
- **Original sources** for the uploaded clips (Thomas Hervé Studio site, the thumbnail-strip and cascading-slider authors, HIRUKI) are not linked from Cosmos and were not traced. The moves are described from the clips.
- **CGI vs photo**: TB-LAB 008 and the Lightmap study are renders. All other photo picks look photographic, but their provenance was not checked beyond the Cosmos caption or uploader.
- Mobile (390 px) was not checked for any live site in this pass.
