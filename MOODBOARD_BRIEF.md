# Gunther Werks Miami — brief for moodboards and concepts

**Project.** The official Gunther Werks dealer website for Miami, run by Prestige Imports (15050 Biscayne Blvd, North Miami Beach). A trimmed, corporate-style brand site in our own design. Not a copy of guntherwerks.com, and not a dealer template.

**The brand.** Gunther Werks is a California atelier in Huntington Beach. It re-engineers the air-cooled Porsche 993, the last air-cooled 911, in carbon fibre and makes over 90% of components in-house. Its engines are 4.0-litre flat-sixes developed with Rothsport Racing. Production runs are tiny. The name honours Gunther Wendt, the NASA engineer known for exacting standards on Apollo. Founder: Peter Nam. Their words: "reengineered and remastered", "not a manufacturer — an atelier".

**The cars.**
- Current programs:
  - F-26: 26 cars, slantnose, swan-neck wing, about 1,000 hp twin-turbo.
  - Turbo: 75 cars.
  - GWR: 40 cars, naturally aspirated, 9,000 rpm.
  - GWX: by invitation.
- Special editions: GXR-EVO (15), Brundle Edition (5), Thermal Edition (5), Project End Game (1 of 1).
- Completed: Coupe (the original 400R, 25) and Speedster (25).
- Pre-owned: Provenance Succession, cars re-validated by the factory.

**Audience.** Collectors and serious enthusiasts in South Florida and on the East Coast. They know the 993 and they buy rarity, craft and engineering, not flash. The action is a private enquiry with a named person, Javier Roque.

**The idea.** Every Gunther Werks is the same 993, remastered. Hold the silhouette still (one profile, one floor, one scale) and let the eye find what the atelier changed: the nose, the wing, the wheels, the intakes. Desire comes from the difference on a constant base.

**Look and feel.**
- Strict black and white. Colour comes only from the cars and the light.
- Dark studio cove: one coloured rim light (warm amber or red), haze, glossy floor, a small car in a large empty field.
- A second register: bright white seamless studio, catalogue-clean, the same left-facing side profile for every car.
- Golden-hour California roads and circuits; macro craft (carbon weave, billet aluminium, the machined key, the engine).
- Mood: held breath, precision, engineered calm. One moment of speed: the F-26 at Goodwood.
- Typography: an uppercase humanist sans in the Gill Sans family, with big condensed numerals for figures.
- Accents only as Gunther Werks uses them: gold "by invitation" on black, program reds sparingly.

**Page, top to bottom (each section one full screen).**
1. Hero: the F-26 in the dark cove with the wordmark.
2. "House Lights": the studio lights come up and the four programs stand side by side at one scale.
3. The atelier.
4. Goodwood: a scroll-driven film of the F-26 passing.
5. Provenance Succession.
6. News & Events.
7. Miami contact and enquiry.
8. Footer.

**Avoid.**
- Generic dark luxury: black, thin caps, gold, one render.
- Gold as decoration.
- Neon, glassmorphism, gradients, fake tech graphics (crosshairs, HUDs).
- Cars on mixed backgrounds or angles in card grids.
- Text over cars.
- Lifestyle clichés: yachts, champagne, models posing with cars.
- Miami clichés: palm neon, pink and teal.
- Anything that copies the Czinger, Pagani or Lamborghini sites.
